Our Process

Transparent from briefing to walkthrough.

The studio experience maps to how we actually build — discovery, feasibility, written scope, craft, QC, and closeout.

Eight-step studio path

01

Discovery & space briefing

We learn the space, constraints, and what success looks like — photos, measurements, and priorities before any design promise.

02

Feasibility & budget bands

Honest project-class guidance ($$ / $$$ / $$$$) based on scope and materials — directional bands, not fake precise bids from a photo.

03

Scope & written plan

Written scope, milestones, and inclusions/exclusions so everyone shares the same definition of done.

04

Materials & detailing

Product selections, detailing decisions, and lead times locked before crews mobilize.

05

Site prep & protection

Protect adjacent finishes, stage materials, and set the job for clean, controlled work.

06

Build & craft execution

Trade work with schedule control, daily communication, and quality checks at each critical layer.

07

QC & change clarity

Documented progress, change orders when scope shifts, and quality control before finish milestones.

08

Walkthrough & closeout

Final walkthrough, punch list, and documentation — then handoff you can live with.

Delivery phases

01

Preconstruction & feasibility

Space, requirements, budget, and timeline before design commitment.

02

Architecture, engineering & permitting

Architectural, MEP, and health department coordination as needed.

03

Construction & build-out

Quality-driven work, clear communication, and schedule control.

04

Project closeout & opening support

Inspections, punch list, and documentation for a clean handoff.

Project standards

Written scope

Clear inclusions, exclusions, and assumptions before work starts.

Milestones

Named checkpoints tied to the build sequence — not vague “phases.”

Progress documentation

Photos and notes at critical layers so invisible work is visible.

Change orders

Scope changes documented and priced before they become surprises.

Quality control

Inspection at substrate, install, and finish — not only at the end.

Walkthrough

Structured closeout with punch list and owner sign-off.
